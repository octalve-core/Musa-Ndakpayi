Approved assets will be placed here with original provenance, permitted use and naming conventions.

- `candidate-portrait.webp` (authentic approved high-resolution image)
- `candidate-hero.webp` (approved landscape campaign image)
- `campaign-logo.svg` or `campaign-logo.webp` (approved campaign identity)
- `party-mark.webp` (party mark only with usage permission)

No portrait or logo is invented during the initial website build.
