# Required before final public release

The initial site intentionally makes no unsourced claim of electoral victory, coalition strength or project delivery.

- [ ] Candidate approves official full name and public title (INEC currently lists USMAN MUSA NDAKPAYI)
- [ ] Campaign provides a high-resolution authentic portrait, hero image, logo, colour identity permissions and release rights
- [ ] Biography signed off: dates, education, career, work and achievements with attributable evidence; check name order, degree and chronology against `docs/BIOGRAPHY-FACT-CHECK.md`
- [ ] Candidate approves each specific legislative promise and final manifesto PDFs; distinguish political proposals from bills already sponsored
- [ ] Campaign verifies contact email, phone number, office address and active social-media profiles
- [ ] Organisers confirm public event dates, venue and permission to publish
- [ ] Named supporters consent to publication of authentic testimonials before any such feature
- [ ] Data controller provides contact details, privacy policy, retention periods and lawful basis before activating registrations, mailing lists, volunteer forms or analytics
- [ ] Domain, SEO base URL, robots indexing, sitemap and Open Graph preview are configured
- [ ] Performance, accessibility and responsive tests pass; no broken remote images
- [ ] Build and deploy production once after checks; avoid Vercel preview exhaustion

## Candidate-content policy

The existing site displays reported positions with links to originating articles. Legislative measures are written as **possible approaches**, not as promises made by the candidate or completed actions. Do not erase these distinctions during future copy revisions without documentary approval.
